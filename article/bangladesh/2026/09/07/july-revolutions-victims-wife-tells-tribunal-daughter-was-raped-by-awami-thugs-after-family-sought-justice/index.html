July Revolution's victim’s wife tells tribunal daughter was raped by Awami thugs after family sought justice

Staff Correspondent 7 সেপ্টেম্বর 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

The wife of a man killed during the July Revolution has told the International Crimes Tribunal that her daughter was allegedly raped after the family sought justice for her husband’s death and later died by suicide.

Testifying before International Crimes Tribunal-1, Ruma Begum said her husband, Jasim Uddin, was shot near Allah Karim Mosque in Mohammadpur on July 19, 2024.

She alleged that Awami League and Chhatra League activists, alongside police, attacked a procession of students and other protesters after Friday prayers.

Jasim suffered a gunshot wound to his chest and severe injuries to his fingers. He died at Shaheed Suhrawardy Medical College Hospital on July 29 after 10 days of treatment.

Ruma alleged that police initially refused to release his body unless the family filed a case. He was eventually buried on July 31.

She said she travelled to Dhaka in March 2025 to give a statement to tribunal investigators over her husband’s killing.

তার জবানবন্দি অনুযায়ী, স্থানীয় আওয়ামী লীগ কর্মীরা পরবর্তীতে তার বড় মেয়ে লামিয়াকে টার্গেট করে, এইচএসসি পরীক্ষার্থী।

Ruma alleged that Lamia was raped on March 18 while returning from visiting her father’s grave.

তিনি বলেন, তার মেয়ে পরে কথিত হামলার পর গুরুতর মানসিক যন্ত্রণা ভোগ করে আত্মহত্যা করে মারা যায়।

ট্রাইব্যুনালে রুমা বলেন, “আমি আমার স্বামী ও আমার সন্তানের বিচার চাই।
