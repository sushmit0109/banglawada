‘Uncle, I have been shot. Save me’: Witness recalls friend’s final plea at tribunal

Staff Correspondent 25 আগস্ট 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

At the International Crimes Tribunal, a prosecution witness described how protests after student deaths in Rangpur and Chattogram spread nationwide before being met with lethal force in Dhaka. He said his friend died after being shot and delayed in an ambulance, hospitals withheld bodies on orders, and former ministers incited the use of lethal weapons and curfew against unarmed demonstrators.

A prosecution witness on Monday recounted before the International Crimes Tribunal his experiences of curfew, shootings and mass killings as part of the trial proceedings over crimes against humanity committed during the 2024 July Uprising.

Md Sakib Ahmed Tulon gave his testimony before a judicial panel of International Crimes Tribunal-1, headed by Justice Mohitul Haq Enam Chowdhury, in the case against former ministers – banned Awami League Presidium Member Qamrul Islam and Workers Party President Rashed Khan Menon.

Qamrul and Menon were brought to the tribunal from jail in the morning. Prosecutors Mizanul Islam, Gazi MH Tamim, Moinul Karim, Abdus Sattar Palowan and others were present during the proceedings.

In his deposition, Sakib, 26, a second-year English student at UITS University, described how the streets were flooded with the blood of students and members of the public and spoke about those he said were responsible for the “hellish” killings.

He said the movement spread across the country after the deaths of Abu Sayed in Rangpur and Wasim Akram in Chattogram on July 16, 2024.

On July 18, he took to the streets in front of Badda Post Office Road and BRAC University. Police dispersed the protesters by firing tear gas and rubber bullets and shot dead a student that day.

The following day, Friday, July 19, he joined protesters in front of BRAC University with his friends Maruf, Faisal and Rajib.

Sakib said, “We saw police and BGB shooting anyone heading towards the Rampura Bridge as soon as they saw them. We saw the bloody bodies of five people lying in front of the barricade. At one point, amid the deafening sound of gunfire, my friend Maruf collapsed on the pavement before my eyes. He had been shot in the abdomen. He was saying, ‘Uncle, I have been shot, save me...’ As he said this, he became unconscious.”

Sakib said he tried to stop the bleeding by pressing on Maruf’s wound, leaving his own hands and watch covered in blood.

Maruf was initially given first aid at a local hospital, but as the bleeding did not stop, they set out for Dhaka Medical College Hospital. However, police and BGB kept the ambulance stopped near Rampura Bridge for about half an hour.

When the ambulance reached near Shantinagar Bridge, Maruf breathed his last in Sajib’s lap.

Upon reaching Dhaka Medical College Hospital, they saw bodies everywhere and heard the cries of people who had been shot. When relatives wanted to take the bodies away, the hospital authorities refused to hand them over, citing instructions they had received.

After the curfew was imposed at night, police again stopped their rickshaw in front of Shilpakala and Ramna police stations and threatened to shoot them.

In his deposition, Sakib further said that in the following days, armed cadres of the Awami League, Jubo League and Chhatra League, along with police, attacked their processions in Badda, Rampura and Bangla Motor with crude bombs, bricks and weapons.

Finally, on August 5, they again took to the streets as part of the “Long March to Dhaka” programme.

While they were stationed in Badda and Shahbagh, they learned between 12pm and 12:30pm that then Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina had resigned and fled the country.

তার জবানবন্দি শেষে, সাকিব বলেছিলেন যে তারা পরে মিডিয়া রিপোর্ট থেকে জানতে পেরেছেন যে শেখ হাসিনা, রাশেদ খান মেনন এবং কামরুল ইসলাম 19 জুলাই গণভবনে একটি সভায় উপস্থিত ছিলেন, যেখানে তারা কারফিউ জারি করার সিদ্ধান্ত নিয়েছিলেন এবং ছাত্র ও জনসাধারণের উপর গণহত্যা চালানোর পরামর্শ দিয়েছিলেন।

জুলাই বিদ্রোহের অংশগ্রহণকারী "মানবতার বিরুদ্ধে জঘন্য অপরাধের" পরিকল্পনা এবং আদেশ জারি করার পিছনে যারা ছিল তাদের জন্য দৃষ্টান্তমূলক শাস্তি দাবি করে তার জবানবন্দি শেষ করেছিল।

৩০ এপ্রিল, আদালত আনুষ্ঠানিকভাবে অভিযুক্ত সাবেক মন্ত্রীদের বিরুদ্ধে অভিযোগ গঠন করে বিচার শুরুর নির্দেশ দেন।

রাষ্ট্রপক্ষে বলা হয়েছে, শেখ হাসিনার নেতৃত্বাধীন সরকারকে ক্ষমতায় রাখতে কামরুল ও মেনন বিভিন্ন উস্কানিমূলক বক্তব্য দিয়েছেন। আওয়ামী লীগ এবং 14-দলীয় জোটের সিনিয়র পদে অধিষ্ঠিত, তারা প্রাণঘাতী অস্ত্র ব্যবহার এবং নিরস্ত্র ছাত্র এবং জনসাধারণের সদস্যদের বিরুদ্ধে কারফিউ আরোপের জন্য প্ররোচিত করেছিল।

Demonstrators were shot, leaving 23 people dead in and around the capital’s Badda area. Many others were injured.
