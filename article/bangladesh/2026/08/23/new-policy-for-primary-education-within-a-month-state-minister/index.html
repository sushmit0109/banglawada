New policy for primary education within a month: State Minister

UNB 23 আগস্ট 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

ঢাকায় এক প্রশিক্ষণ কর্মশালায় প্রতিমন্ত্রী ববি হাজ্জাজ বলেছেন, বাংলাদেশের সরকারি প্রাথমিক বিদ্যালয়গুলোতে ব্যবস্থাপনা, মনিটরিং ও জবাবদিহিতা জোরদার করতে এক মাসের মধ্যে একটি নতুন প্রাথমিক শিক্ষা নীতি প্রণয়ন করা হবে। নীতিটি সরকারী নির্বাচনী প্রতিশ্রুতি এবং পদ্ধতিগত সংস্কারের মাধ্যমে একটি শিক্ষিত জাতি গঠনের প্রধানমন্ত্রীর লক্ষ্যকে সমর্থন করে সকল স্তরের কর্মকর্তা ও কর্মচারীদের গাইড করবে।

State Minister for Primary and Mass Education Bobby Hajjaj today said a new policy will be formulated within a month to ensure effective management, monitoring and accountability of primary education activities.

"All officials and employees in the primary education sector will discharge their duties in line with the new policy, while activities of government primary schools will also be conducted accordingly," he said.

The state minister was speaking as the chief guest at a daylong training workshop titled "Conducting Class Activities, Monitoring, Accountability and Reform of Administrative Structure in Primary Education of Bangladesh" at the Shilpakala Academy here.

"We will formulate the new policy within a month . . . The primary education family and government primary schools will also be run accordingly," Bobby Hajjaj said.

Highlighting the government's plans for the development of primary education, he said members of the primary education family were working relentlessly to build an educated Bangladesh in line with the Prime Minister's vision.

He said efforts were underway to ensure that the implementation of the government's electoral pledges becomes visible within a short time.

"The primary education family will be the key force in realising the Prime Minister's dream for Bangladesh," he said.

Deputy Director of Primary Education in Barishal Division Nilufar Yasmin presided over the workshop.

Director (Planning and Development) of the Directorate of Primary Education Mirajul Islam Ukil and Deputy Secretary (Administration-1) Mohammad Sakhawat Hossain Sarker attended the event as special guests.

Officials and employees from different levels of the primary education sector attended the workshop, where various aspects of classroom activities, monitoring, accountability and reform of the administrative structure were discussed.
