Forest Dept takes back two elephants for violating custody conditions

Staff Correspondent 24 আগস্ট 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

The Forest Department has reclaimed elephants Nihar Koli and Samrat Bahadur after their custodians allegedly violated strict welfare conditions. Ordered by Environment Minister Abdul Awal Mintoo, the move underscores the government’s zero-tolerance stance on wildlife abuse. Both elephants, previously rescued amid mistreatment and public money collection allegations, will undergo medical checks and close monitoring at Gazipur Safari Park.

The Forest Department has taken two elephants, ‘Nihar Koli’ and ‘Samrat Bahadur’, back into its custody on Monday after their custodians allegedly violated the conditions set for keeping them.

Environment, Forest and Climate Change Minister Abdul Awal Mintoo ordered the move on Monday to ensure the safety, protection and welfare of the elephants, said a press release from the ministry.

“The government is committed to ensuring the welfare, safety and protection of wildlife. If wildlife is handed over to any individual or organisation and the stipulated conditions are violated, necessary action will be taken in accordance with the law and regulations,” the minister said.

Chief Conservator of Forests Md Amir Hossain Chowdhury said they began taking action immediately after receiving the minister’s directive.

“Although the minister is currently in Mongolia to attend the COP-17 conference, he has been regularly keeping track of the matter,” he said.

The female elephant Nihar Koli was taken into custody from the Kamalganj area of Moulvibazar, while the male elephant Samrat was taken from an area near Shilua Tea Garden in Juri upazila.

Upon the two elephant’s arrival at Gazipur Safari Park, veterinary officers and members of a specialised medical board will conduct health examinations. The elephants will then be moved to designated sheds and kept under close observation.

Earlier, the Forest Department handed over the elephants to their owners on August 12 after securing written undertakings that they would not be abused, subjected to unnecessary suffering or used to collect money from the public.

হাতিটির সাথে দুর্ব্যবহার করা এবং মানুষের কাছ থেকে অর্থ আদায় করার অভিযোগের মধ্যে 2024 সালের 31 আগস্ট নারায়ণগঞ্জের ভুলতা থেকে নীহার কলিকে উদ্ধার করা হয়েছিল।

সম্রাটের ব্যাপারটা আরও গুরুতর ছিল। 24 সেপ্টেম্বর, 2024 তারিখে গোপালগঞ্জের কোটালীপাড়ায় তার মাহুত, নজরুল ইসলামকে পদদলিত করার পর হাতিটিকে উদ্ধার করা হয়। বন বিভাগ পরবর্তীতে সম্রাটকে সাফারি পার্কে নিয়ে যায়।

আধিকারিকরা বলেছেন যে সম্রাটকে জনসাধারণের কাছ থেকে অর্থ সংগ্রহের জন্য ব্যবহার করা হয়েছিল বলেও অভিযোগ রয়েছে।
