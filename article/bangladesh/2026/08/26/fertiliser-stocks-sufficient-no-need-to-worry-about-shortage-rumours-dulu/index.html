Fertiliser stocks sufficient, no need to worry about shortage rumours: Dulu

He said legal action would taken against any malpractice, including hoarding and black-marketing

BSS 26 আগস্ট 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

Minister for Disaster Management and Relief Asadul Habib Dulu has urged all to remain vigilant against those who create panic by spreading rumours of an artificial fertiliser crisis.

“The country has adequate stocks of fertiliser to meet farmers' needs. So, there is no need to worry about rumours of fertiliser shortages,” he said.
 
Addressing a review meeting on fertilizer distribution and management at Lalmonirhat Circuit House yesterday, he said legal action would taken against any malpractice, including hoarding and black-marketing, as well as negligence in discharging duty by government officials during the fertiliser season.

Earlier, the government formed a three-member committee to review activities related to the recruitment of fertiliser dealers and the extraction, distribution and sale of fertiliser, and to provide necessary advice and assistance to the Ministry of Agriculture.

The Disaster Management and Relief Minister has been made a member of the committee, according to a notification issued earlier by the Cabinet Division.

লালমনিরহাট জেলা প্রশাসক মোঃ রাশেদুল হক প্রধানের সভাপতিত্বে সভায় আরও বক্তব্য রাখেন লালমনিরহাট জেলা পরিষদের প্রশাসক একেএম মমিনুল হক।

সভায় অন্যান্যের মধ্যে জেলা প্রশাসন, পুলিশ ও কৃষি বিভাগের কর্মকর্তারা এবং জেলার সার ব্যবসায়ী ও ডিলাররা উপস্থিত ছিলেন।
