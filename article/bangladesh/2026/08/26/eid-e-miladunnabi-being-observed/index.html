Eid-e-Miladunnabi being observed

UNB 26 আগস্ট 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

The holy Eid-e-Miladunnabi, commemorating the birth and death of Prophet Hazrat Muhammad (PBUH), is being observed observed across the country on Wednesday with due religious reverence and solemnity.

On this day in 570, the 12th of Rabiul Awal of the Hijri calendar, Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was born in Makkah with divine blessings and messages of peace for mankind.

He also passed away on the same day.

The government and various other organisations have taken elaborate programmes to mark the occasion with due respect.

The national flag has been hoisted atop all government, semi-government and autonomous offices, as well as private buildings.

বিগত বছরের মতো এবারও সরকার রাজধানীর গুরুত্বপূর্ণ সড়ক ও গুরুত্বপূর্ণ স্থানগুলোকে জাতীয় ও রঙিন পতাকা ও কালেমা তাইয়্যেবা লেখা ব্যানার দিয়ে সজ্জিত করেছে।

The Islamic Foundation has organised various religious and cultural programmes including Qirat, Naat recitations, poetry readings, cultural competitions and an Arabic Khutba writing contest.

Islamic Foundation organised a month-long Islamic book fair at the south plaza of Baitul Mukarram National Mosque.

Bangladesh Television (BTV), Bangladesh Betar and other electronic media outlets broadcast special programmes, while newspapers were expected to publish special supplements on the occasion.

Bangladesh missions abroad will also observe the day with due solemnity and respect.

Meanwhile, President Mirza Fakhrul Islam Alamgir and Prime Minister Tarique Rahman issued separate messages extending greetings to Muslims in Bangladesh and around the world on the occasion.

In his message, the President said the Prophet (PBUH), through his life and deeds, set outstanding examples of truth, justice, forgiveness, compassion and respect for human dignity.

তিনি বলেন, "তিনি এতিম, দরিদ্র, অসহায় ও বঞ্চিতদের অধিকার প্রতিষ্ঠা করেছেন। নারী, শিশু, প্রতিবেশী এবং শ্রমজীবী ​​মানুষের অধিকার নিশ্চিত করার বিষয়েও তিনি সুস্পষ্ট দিকনির্দেশনা দিয়েছেন।"

In a world devastated by war, conflict, violence, inequality and intolerance, the Prophet’s ideals of peace, justice, tolerance, love and humanity are more relevant than ever, Mirza Fakhrul said.

He urged all to draw inspiration from the Prophet’s teachings and pledge to shun violence, hatred, division and injustice and work towards building a just society and state founded on peace, harmony, equality and humanity.

In his message, the Prime Minister called upon people to follow the life and teachings of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) to build a peaceful, just and humane society.

He said the Prophet’s life is a living embodiment of the teachings of the Quran and that he set outstanding examples of honesty, trustworthiness, justice, forgiveness, tolerance, compassion and charity.

Tarique Rahman said the Prophet (PBUH) upheld human dignity and rights by transcending divisions based on race, ethnicity, tribe, language and social status.

Referring to ongoing crises around the world, he said wars, conflicts, violence, inequality, intolerance and moral degradation were disrupting peace, security and normal life. “In such a time, the teachings of the Prophet (PBUH) on peace, justice, compassion, forgiveness and humanity are highly relevant,” he said.

The Prime Minister urged people to incorporate those teachings into their personal, family and social lives, saying mutual respect, compassion, tolerance and a sense of justice are essential to building a peaceful, just and humane Bangladesh.

He also prayed for peace, justice, equality, prosperity and humanity in Bangladesh and across the world.
