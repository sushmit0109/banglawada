Hadi murder: Court sets July 15 for further probe report

BSS 28 জুন 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

A Dhaka court on Sunday set July 15 for the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) to submit a report on its further investigation into the sensational murder of Inqilab Mancha spokesperson Sharif Osman Hadi.

Dhaka Additional Chief Metropolitan Magistrate Jashita Islam passed the order as the investigation agency failed to submit the report.

On January 15, 2026, the court directed the CID to conduct further investigation into the murder as the complainant expressed dissatisfaction with the probe and filed a naraji petition against the charge-sheet submitted by the Detective Branch (DB) of police, seeking further investigation.

DB Inspector Foysal Ahmed submitted the charge-sheet on January 6 against 17 accused, including alleged shooter Foisal Karim Masud and his parents-Humayun Kabir and Hashi Begum.

According to the case statement, Foisal Karim Masud and Alamgir Hossain opened fire on Hadi from a battery-run auto-rickshaw on December 12, 2025, and fled the scene.

Inqilab Mancha Member Secretary Abdullah Al Zaber filed the murder case with Paltan Model Police Station on December 14.

Dhaka Metropolitan Magistrate Siddiq Azad on December 20 ordered the inclusion of Section 302 (murder) of the Penal Code in the case, which was initially filed as attempted murder, following Hadi's death on December 18.
