Rana heading to Big Bash?

Sports Desk 7 সেপ্টেম্বর 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

Bangladesh pacer Nahid Rana has attracted interest from several franchises in Australia’s Big Bash League (BBL), however, scheduling conflicts raise doubts over his participation, according to multiple outlets.

Online-based sports news outletস্টেডিয়ামfirst broke the news, reporting that Rana has received offers from Adelaide Strikers and a Melbourne-based franchise.

The next edition of the BBL is set to begin on December 12, 2026, to January 26, 2027. The national team is set to tour South Africa for a three-format series in November-December and talks are underway for a white-ball series against India in early January.

The speedster is still recovering from a Grade 2 left-side strain, which ruled him out of Bangladesh’s tour of Australia last month.

রানা আবার বোলিং শুরু, চোখ অক্টোবর ফিরে
অস্ট্রেলিয়ার ওয়েবস্টার রানার অনুপস্থিতি একটি ভাগ্যবান বিরতি স্বীকার করেছেন

Rana is also set to miss the Asian Games 2026 and is planning to return with the series against Afghanistan in October.

The 23-year-old has attracted global attention with his raw pace and has already plied his trade in overseas franchise leagues – playing for Peshawar Zalmi in the previous edition of the Pakistan Super League (PSL).

He took a four-wicket haul against Australia in a home ODI series in June earlier this year, helping the Tigers notch their first win in the 50-over format against the opponents in 21 years.

Till now, only two players from Bangladesh have featured in the BBL – Shakib Al Hasan and Rishad Hossain.
