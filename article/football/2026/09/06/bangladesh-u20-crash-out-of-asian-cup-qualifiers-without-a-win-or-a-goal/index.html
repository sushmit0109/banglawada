খেলাধুলা

জয়হীন এবং গোলশূন্য, বাংলাদেশ অনূর্ধ্ব-২০ দল এশিয়ান কাপের বাছাইপর্ব থেকে বিদায় নিয়েছে

Sports Desk 6 সেপ্টেম্বর 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

এএফসি অনূর্ধ্ব-২০ এশিয়ান কাপ কোয়ালিফায়ারে বাংলাদেশ তাদের টানা তৃতীয় পরাজয়ের শিকার হয়েছে, রবিবার ভারতের বিপক্ষে ০-৩ হেরে জয় বা গোল না করে তাদের অভিযান শেষ করেছে।

The team’s campaign was disastrous both on and off the field, as after defeats to Uzbekistan (0-2) and Syria (0-3) in the first two games, the Bangladesh Football Federation (BFF) had sacked the team’s head coach Gerard Jones.

Assistant coach Atikur Rahman Misho was flown in from Dhaka to take charge of the team against India, but the change in coach did not bring any respite.

In the match, Danny Laishram opened the scoring in the 25th minute, then Vishal Yadav (60th) and Mohammad Arbash (79th) netted one each in the second half as India eased to a comfortable win.

সিরিয়া হারের পর বরখাস্ত U20 কোচ জোন্স
অনুর্ধ্ব-২০ কোচ জোন্স প্রেসারে অনুপস্থিত, আজ রাতে এশিয়ান কাপ বাছাইপর্বের জন্য রওনা হবে স্কোয়াড

Just a few months back, the team in red and green had defeated India to lift the SAFF U20 Championship in the Maldives.

The team had arrived in Uzbekistan dreaming of qualifying for the main-stage of the AFC U20 Asian Cup for the first time but ended up failing to cross the group stage.

Bangladesh’s buildup to the tournament was chaotic to say the least, with coach Jones, who was hired in June, at the heart of it.

In the month of August, Jones complained of payment delays, and expressed fears about his safety on social media, He even called the police after a reported altercation with Mishu at the team hotel.

In the press conference before the sides’ departure, neither the head coach nor the players were present. Instead, team manager Sameed Quasem conducted the presser.
