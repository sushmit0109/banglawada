পশ্চিম যে কোনো উপায়ে হারানো নেতৃত্ব ফিরে পেতে চাই: পুতিন

Waadaa Desk 11 সেপ্টেম্বর 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

শুক্রবার রাশিয়ার রাষ্ট্রীয় মালিকানাধীন বার্তা সংস্থা তাস জানিয়েছে, ভারতের নয়াদিল্লিতে ব্রিকস বিজনেস ফোরামে বক্তৃতাকালে রাশিয়ার প্রেসিডেন্ট ভ্লাদিমির পুতিন বলেছেন, পশ্চিমারা অস্বস্তিকর পদ্ধতি সহ যেকোনো উপায়ে তাদের হারানো নেতৃত্ব ফিরে পাওয়ার চেষ্টা করছে।

"But, unfortunately, our Western competitors are currently trying to regain their former leadership by any means possible. Today, this competition is sometimes taking on unsavory forms - something a colleague from Iran also just mentioned here. Moreover, these unsavory forms are manifesting at the state level," he said.

Blaming the West for impacting logistics, destroying infrastructure and seizing ships in a bid to maintain the so-called status quo, Putin also lambasted Western-led sanctions, saying the embargoes were being used to “threaten those who are unwilling to act in someone else’s interests but protect their own national interests."

He also noted that strong and reliable business ties within BRICS form the basis of international relations, making them more resilient.

"BRICS countries, home to about half the world’s population, have vast, dynamic domestic markets. Cities in our countries are developing rapidly and growing, urbanisation is proceeding swiftly, and national companies are increasingly making their mark as global players."

He expressed hope that the BRICS new investment platform will be supported by other members of the association.
