Rubio says sanctions instead of new strikes against Iran for now: Axios

আরেক মার্কিন কর্মকর্তা বলেছেন, মধ্যবর্তী নির্বাচন না হওয়া পর্যন্ত সামরিক বিকল্প টেবিলের বাইরে রয়েছে

Waadaa Desk 26 আগস্ট 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

Axios reports that Marco Rubio has told allied foreign ministers the US will rely on economic tools rather than new military strikes against Iran, at least until after the midterm elections. Measures include a naval blockade, tougher Treasury sanctions and efforts to keep oil exports flowing. Officials argue the pressure campaign is crippling Iran’s economy and could push the regime back to negotiations.

The US is not expected to initiate any new strikes against Iran “for the time being”, focusing instead on other means of pressure, including sanctions announced this week, Secretary of State Marco Rubio told several of his foreign counterparts, according to a report by Axios.

As per the report, in phone calls with foreign ministers from several allied countries, Rubio chalked out the alternatives ways being mulled to ramp up pressure on Iran.

These include Avoiding military action against Iran for the time being increasing economic pressure through a US naval blockade and a new Treasury Department sanctions push, alongside transporting as much oil as possible through the strait into global energy markets.

"The Iranian economy is in free fall and the regime's military has been decimated," State Department spokesman Tommy Pigott told Axios, "and we are cutting off every financial lifeline the regime has remaining."

"The President has been clear that Iran cannot have a nuclear weapon, and that he will use the tools necessary to ensure that objective is accomplished," he added.

Another US official, highlighting the “large volumes of oil being transported out of the strait”, said, “Right now we are not negotiating with Iran. We are squeezing them. The pressure could drive the Iranians back to the table.”

Furthermore, Axios cites another US official as saying the military option would be off the table until at least after the midterm elections in the first week of November.

Rubio reportedly believes that the US is suffocating Iran with the naval blockade.

Although major combat operations are ruled out for now, the US will strike if Iran attacks first, Rubio is believed to have told his counterparts.

ট্রাম্পকে সতর্ক করা হয়েছিল ইরানের যুদ্ধে পাল্টা আঘাত আসতে পারে। যাই হোক সে এগিয়ে গেল

The Axios report also cites another US official, who believes that by clearing mines from the Strait of Hormuz, Iran’s main source of leverage has been neutralised.

“The Iranians have lost control over the strait. Now the US controls it," one US official told Axios.

Al Arabiya, a Saudi-affiliated TV station earlier reported that before the Tehran visit by Pakistan's army chief, Field Marshal Asim Munir, on Sunday for talks with the Iranian leadership, he had spoken to Trump and suggested a new proposal for the Iranians.

It should be noted that Pakistan has been attempting to broker peace between the two nations.

Earlier on Monday, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent had announced that the US will impose punishing sanctions on countries and entities that do business with Iran.

এটিকে "অপারেশন ইকোনমিক আউটকাস্ট" হিসেবে অভিহিত করে এক সংবাদ সম্মেলনে বেসেন্ট বলেন, "মার্কিন যুক্তরাষ্ট্রের অবস্থান নিয়ে কোনো অস্পষ্টতা থাকবে না। এই হত্যাকাণ্ডের সাথে যে কোনো ধরনের অর্থনৈতিক সম্পৃক্ততা দায়ী ব্যক্তিদের আমেরিকান ক্ষমতার সম্পূর্ণ নাগালের কাছে উন্মোচিত করবে।"

তিনি অবশ্য সম্মতির জন্য কোনো সময়সীমা দেননি।

Following Bessent's announcement, China – which  accounts for more than 80% of Iranian oil shipments –  said its cooperation with Iran has always taken place within the framework of international law.

Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson Lin Jian said China-Iran cooperation “should not be disrupted or undermined.”

He also said Beijing would closely monitor developments and take necessary steps to protect its interests, while reiterating China's opposition to what it considers “illegal unilateral sanctions.”
