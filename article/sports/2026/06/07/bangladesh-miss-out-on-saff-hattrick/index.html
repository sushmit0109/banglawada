Bangladesh miss out on SAFF hattrick

Staff Correspondent 7 জুন 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

Bangladesh’s hopes of lifting a third straight SAFF Women’s Championship title were crushed after they suffered a 1-3 defeat against India in the final at the Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium in Goa, India on Saturday.

Ritu Porna Chakma scored Bangladesh’s only goal of the match in the added time of the first half to level the score 1-1 after India’s Pyari Xaxa gave the hosts the lead in the 42nd minute.

But Bangladesh’s relief proved to be short lived as India regained the lead in the first minute of the second half, with Sanfida Nongram finding the back of the net with a header.

Peter Butler’s charges tried to equalise but could not create enough chances. Lynda Kom then hit the final nail in Bangladesh’s coffin with an 82-minute goal.

Bangladesh had won the previous two SAFF titles in 2022 and 2024.

With the victory, India won their sixth SAFF championship in women’s football.
