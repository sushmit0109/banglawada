খেলাধুলা

Single rooms for Bangladesh cricketers, cruise ship for eight other disciplines in Asian Games, says BOA

Staff Correspondent 7 সেপ্টেম্বর 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

Bangladesh’s cricketers will enjoy mostly single-room accommodation at the Asian Games in Japan, while eight other Bangladeshi teams will have to stay on a cruise ship amid a housing crunch, revealed a Bangladesh Olympic Association (BOA) official on Monday.

Bangladesh will send 22 teams across different disciplines to the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games, which will be held from September 19 to October 4.

The Bangladesh cricket contingent has secured 19 rooms for its 41 players and officials, covering both the men’s and women’s teams. Almost all members of the cricket delegation will have rooms to themselves.

“India [cricket contingent] is sharing every room. If they send 25 or 22 people, they will not get 20 rooms. Bangladesh has been allocated 19 rooms. Everyone will stay in single rooms, except one person who will have to share,” BOA vice-president Major (retd) Imroze Ahmed told reporters at a press conference at the BOA auditorium.

Eight of our teams will stay on a cruise ship. It has not yet been decided which decks they will stay on or what arrangements will be made. We will know after we arrive. Our remaining teams will stay in hotels
Major (retd) Imroze Ahmed, BOA vice-president

The Bangladesh cricket teams will compete in separate tournaments, with the women’s event scheduled for September 17-22 and the men’s competition from September 24 to October 3.

Imroz said Bangladesh’s cricket team had avoided the worst of the crisis because arrangements had been made early.

"কেউ একটি ভাল কাজ করেছে এবং এটি ব্যবস্থা করার জন্য এক ধাপ এগিয়ে গেছে। আমাদের ক্রিকেট দল এর কারণে ক্ষতিগ্রস্থ হচ্ছে না," তিনি বলেছিলেন।

But not all athletes from Bangladesh would be as lucky as the cricketers, as out of the 22 teams to be sent across different disciplines, eight will have to stay on a cruise ship, revealed Imroze.

"Eight of Bangladesh’s disciplines at this year’s Asian Games will be accommodated on a cruise ship. Members of the archery, gymnastics, judo, kabaddi, weightlifting, wrestling, table tennis and wushu teams will stay there.

"অন্যান্য ডিসিপ্লিনের ক্রীড়াবিদদের বিভিন্ন হোটেল বা রিসোর্টে থাকার ব্যবস্থা করা হবে," ইমরোজ বলেন।

'তাদের থাকার কোথাও নেই': জাপান এশিয়ান গেমসে দেরিতে আসা খেলোয়াড়দের নিজস্ব বাসস্থান খুঁজে নিতে বলেছে

The accommodation arrangements have become a major issue ahead of the Games, with no conventional athletes’ village being built for the event. Around 17,000 athletes and officials are expected to attend.

পরিবর্তে, অংশগ্রহণকারীদের হোটেল, ক্রুজ জাহাজ এবং অস্থায়ী কন্টেইনার-স্টাইল সুবিধাগুলিতে স্থান দেওয়া হবে, যা জাপান জুড়ে উপলব্ধ বাসস্থানের উপর যথেষ্ট চাপ সৃষ্টি করবে।

Imroz said securing hotel rooms had become extremely difficult as the Games approached, describing the shortage as a nationwide problem.

“If you ask for a room now, I might not even be able to give you a toilet, because there are no hotel rooms available anywhere in Japan,” he said.
