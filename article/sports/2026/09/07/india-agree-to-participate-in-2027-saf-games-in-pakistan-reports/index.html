ভারত পাকিস্তানে 2027 সালের SAF গেমসে অংশ নিতে 'সম্মত': রিপোর্ট

Sports Desk 7 সেপ্টেম্বর 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

পাকিস্তানে 2027 দক্ষিণ এশিয়ান ফেডারেশন (SAF) গেমসে অংশ নিতে ভারত নীতিগতভাবে সম্মত হয়েছে, পাকিস্তানি মিডিয়া রিপোর্ট অনুসারে, আঞ্চলিক বহু-ক্রীড়া ইভেন্টের জন্য সম্ভাব্য একটি বড় বাধা সাফ করে।

Pakistan is scheduled to host the Games from April 4 to 11, 2027, marking its return as host after a 23-year gap.

According to Pakistani outlet The News, Pakistan Olympic Association (POA) Secretary General Khalid Mehmood said India was represented at a high-level meeting of the South Asian Olympic Association and accepted Pakistan’s invitation to participate.

According to Khalid, India has agreed in principle at the relevant regional sporting level, while details of its contingent will be communicated in January 2027.

Pakistan has stepped up preparations for the Games, with a recent meeting chaired by Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar reviewing arrangements for venues, security, transportation, accommodation and medical facilities.

The Games are expected to feature competitions across 28 sports in Islamabad, Lahore and Peshawar.

Islamabad will host 15 sports, including squash, tennis and martial arts, while Lahore will stage 10, including swimming, cricket and hockey. Peshawar will host three disciplines.

Security remains a key concern, with thousands of athletes, coaches, officials, technical staff, spectators and international media personnel expected to travel to Pakistan.

Pakistan last hosted the SAF Games in Islamabad from March 29 to April 7, 2004. The 2027 edition will therefore mark the country’s return as host after 23 years.
