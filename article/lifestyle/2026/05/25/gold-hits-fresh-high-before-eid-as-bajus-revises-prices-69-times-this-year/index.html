Gold hits fresh high before Eid as BAJUS revises prices 69 times this year

Staff Correspondent 25 মে 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

Gold prices in Bangladesh have surged again ahead of Eid-ul-Azha, with the Bangladesh Jewellers Association (BAJUS) announcing a fresh hike amid continued volatility in the local and international bullion markets.

In the first five months of 2026 alone, BAJUS has revised gold prices 69 times, including 37 increases and 32 decreases, underlining continued instability in the country’s bullion market ahead of the Eid shopping season.

The latest revision, effective from 10:00am Monday, has pushed the price of 22-carat gold — the highest-quality standard in the domestic market — to Tk 238,121 per bhori.

Under the revised rates, the price of 22-carat gold has increased by Tk 2,158 per bhori. A bhori of 21-carat gold is now selling at Tk 227,331, while 18-carat gold costs Tk 194,847. Traditional-method gold has been fixed at Tk 158,689 per bhori. One bhori equals 11.664 grams.

BAJUS said the latest adjustment was made after reviewing the overall market situation and the rising price of “tejabi” or pure gold in the local market.

The increase came just two days after the association reduced prices by the same amount, reflecting sharp fluctuations in the bullion trade before Eid-ul-Azha.

Silver prices have also increased alongside gold. The price of 22-carat silver rose by Tk 117 per bhori to Tk 5,774, while 21-carat silver now costs Tk 5,540 per bhori.

According to BAJUS data, gold prices were adjusted 93 times throughout 2025, reflecting one of the most volatile periods in Bangladesh’s bullion market in recent years.

Since the last Eid-ul-Azha, gold prices have undergone more than 100 revisions, with repeated hikes pushing the metal to record highs several times.

Market analysts said the volatility has been driven by rising international gold prices, fluctuations in the US dollar exchange rate, geopolitical tensions, and growing local demand during festival and wedding seasons.

Earlier this year, gold prices crossed the Tk 260,000 mark per bhori for the first time in Bangladesh after BAJUS announced multiple consecutive hikes. However, the market also witnessed frequent downward revisions in March and April as international bullion prices temporarily eased.
