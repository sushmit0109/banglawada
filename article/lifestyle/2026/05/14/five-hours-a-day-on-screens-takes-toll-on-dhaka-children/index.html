Five hours a day on screens takes toll on Dhaka children

Staff Correspondent 14 মে 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

Children in Dhaka are spending an average of nearly five hours a day on digital screens, and many are facing sleep problems, obesity, headaches and mental health issues, according to a new study by icddr,b.

The study surveyed 420 children aged 6 to 14 from six schools in Dhaka between 2022 and 2024. It found that 83% of the children spent more than two hours a day on smartphones, televisions, tablets, computers and gaming devices, far above the internationally recommended limit for recreational screen time.

On average, the children used screens for 4.6 hours a day.

Researchers found that children who spent more than two hours a day on screens slept 7.3 hours a night, below the recommended 8 to 10 hours for their age group.

More than one-third of the children reported eye problems, while 80% frequently suffered from headaches. About 14% were overweight or obese, with higher rates among those with heavy screen use.

The study also found that around two in five children showed signs of mental health problems, including anxiety, hyperactivity, behavioural difficulties and emotional distress.

Lead researcher Dr Shahria Hafiz Kakon said parents should watch for signs such as late sleeping, headaches, irritability, reduced outdoor activity and poor concentration.

World Health Organization recommends limiting recreational screen time for school-aged children to around two hours a day.

The researchers said technology does not need to be banned, but children should have healthy limits. They recommended more outdoor play, proper sleep, device-free family time and activities such as reading and debates.
