Energypacee hosts capacity-building session on solar business strategies in Bangladesh

Staff Correspondent 14 মে 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

Energypac Infrastructure & Development Ltd. recently organized a training session titled “Solar Business Strategies in Bangladesh Market” to strengthen employees’ understanding of the country’s growing solar energy sector.

The two-hour session brought together officials from relevant business units and focused on the commercial and technical aspects of solar projects in Bangladesh.

The training covered a wide range of topics, including solar energy opportunities and market potential, the working principles of solar systems, EPC project technical design, solar angle optimization for maximum power generation, project pricing and cost-reduction techniques, advanced materials and implementation strategies, and the latest innovations in solar technology.

Participants also received insights into different types of LONGi solar panels and Back Contact Technology, one of the industry’s most advanced solutions for improving efficiency and performance.

The session was conducted by Mr. Ahsan, Senior Manager at LONGi Green Energy Technology, who shared practical knowledge and industry perspectives on emerging trends and business strategies in the solar market.

As Bangladesh seeks to diversify its energy mix and enhance energy security, such upskilling programs are expected to play an important role in building the technical and commercial capabilities needed to support the growth of the renewable energy sector.

Through such capacity-building initiatives, Energypac continues to invest in the professional development of its workforce while reinforcing its commitment to promoting sustainable and innovative energy solutions in Bangladesh.
