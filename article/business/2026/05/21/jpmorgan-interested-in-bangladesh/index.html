JPMorgan interested in Bangladesh

Staff Correspondent 21 মে 2026 মূল ইংরেজি পড়ুন ↗

Staff Correspondent

Global investors, including JPMorgan Chase & Co., are interested in investing in Bangladesh, but they need confidence in the country’s financial reporting, Finance and Planning Minister Amir Khosru Mahmud Chowdhury said on Wednesday.

Speaking at a summit on trustworthy financial reporting in Dhaka, Khosru said he meets fund managers every day and many are keen to enter Bangladesh.

But he said investment will not come unless audit reports are accurate and reliable.

Khosru said Bangladesh’s financial system has long suffered due to weak institutions, poor banking discipline, capital flight, companies being listed on the stock market with incorrect information.

“These irregularities have created a capital shortage in both the private sector and the banking sector,” he said.

He said Bangladesh is now at a crossroads and restoring investor confidence will depend on stronger institutions, transparency and accountability.

Rashed Al Mahmud Titumir said past financial misreporting hurt genuine businesses while rewarding those who provided false information.

He said audit firms must act independently and warned that strict punishment will be imposed for deliberate misreporting and manipulated valuations.
